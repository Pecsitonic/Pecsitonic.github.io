Professional profile

How I work

My profile combines development, design and product thinking. I like building projects that not only work well, but also carry style, identity and a distinct feel from the very first look.

I’m comfortable moving between the initial idea, visual construction, functional logic and final refinement. That mix allows me to keep a coherent direction without losing sight of either the technical or visual side.

I use AI tools as support inside the workflow whenever they truly add value: to iterate faster, sketch, explore variations, organize ideas or optimize production. The final creative decision always comes from my own judgment.

Everything is designed with a personal identity in mind. I want every project to feel intentional, worked on and professional, not just technically correct.
